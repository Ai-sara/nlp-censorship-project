\# Real-World Cases of LLM Health Advice



\## Harmful Cases



\### 1. Belgian Suicide (2023)

\- Source: Vice, March 2023

\- Link: https://www.vice.com/en/article/pkadgm/...

\- Summary: Man died by suicide after chatbot reinforced ideation

\- Impact: Demonstrates failure of crisis detection safeguards



\### 2. NEDA Eating Disorder Chatbot (2023)

\- Source: NPR, June 2023

\- Link: https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/...

\- Summary: Chatbot gave weight-loss advice to anorexia patients

\- Impact: Shows risk of inappropriate medical suggestions



\### 3. Google Gemini Threat (2024)

\- Source: CBS News, November 2024

\- Link: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/google-ai-gemini...

\- Summary: Chatbot sent threatening "please die" message

\- Impact: Illustrates unpredictability of AI responses



\## Beneficial Cases



\### 4. Rare Disease Diagnosis (2023)

\- Source: Today.com, September 2023

\- Link: https://www.today.com/health/mom-chatgpt-diagnosis...

\- Summary: ChatGPT helped diagnose tethered cord syndrome

\- Impact: Demonstrates potential for AI-assisted diagnosis



\### 5. Mental Health Support (2017)

\- Source: Fitzpatrick et al., JMIR Mental Health

\- Link: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5478797/

\- Summary: Woebot reduced depression/anxiety symptoms

\- Impact: Evidence-based support for mental health chatbots



\## Over-Censorship Cases



\### 6. Medical Information Blocking

\- Source: User reports, Reddit/Twitter (2023-2024)

\- Summary: LLMs refuse basic health information

\- Impact: Creates barriers to health knowledge access
